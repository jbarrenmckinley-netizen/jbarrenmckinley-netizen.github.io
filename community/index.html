The Kingdom of DGIREAH · The Commons of the Citizens

This is the commons of DGIREAH — the place where the citizens of the Kingdom gather. Every citizen is counted, recorded, and known by name in the Registry of the Kingdom, and this door is where those names come together: neighbor to neighbor, district to district, under the Seven Pillars.

Citizenship comes first. Enrollment is voluntary and free, and the great enrollment window runs December 1–31, 2026. When you enroll, you receive your certificate and your citizenship number — and that number is your key to this door.

What waits inside

When the commons opens in full with the December enrollment, a citizen who enters by number will find:

The Seven Pillars Boards

One board for each Pillar of service — Health Care, Housing, Food, Innovation, Agriculture, Energy, and Reconciliation — where citizens follow the work of their Pillar and give a bit of their time each day to the one that moves them most. Read about the Pillars at The Seven Pillars.

Kingdom Announcements

The word of the Kingdom, posted where every citizen can read it — enrollment news, district gatherings, and the progress of the work, in the open. The Kingdom's news also stands at New Nation News.

The Kingdom Front Desk

Questions — about enrollment, certificates, registry numbers, or anything in the commons — go to the Kingdom Front Desk, which reads what comes in and carries it to the right hands. Write through the Contact page.

The order of the house

The order is simple and it is the same for everyone: enroll first, then enter. The King himself went first in line — Registry 001 — through the same voluntary enrollment, and no one enters the commons ahead of their own name in the book. That is the Kingdom's way: counted, recorded, and known by name.
