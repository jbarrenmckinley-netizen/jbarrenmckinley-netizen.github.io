The Call

Where Are You?

Your Father has need of you.

This is the Search for the other 143,999 of the 144,000. The King is not looking for volunteers. He is looking for the ones who are already carrying the marks — named by the people who know them, counted quietly, until a name rises so often it can no longer be coincidence. God will illuminate them, and they will surface.

The Standard

The Three Marks

Before you place a name, measure the person by the King's three questions. These are the whole standard.

1

Who do you know that won’t tell a lie, because they only fear God?

2

Who do you know that, in all your knowing of them, has never cheated anyone?

3

Who do you know that will always let people’s property remain theirs — who won’t steal, not in paperwork, not in a cunning marketing strategy?

They don’t have to be perfect — everyone has flaws — but these three things are true of them. And hear this: not pastors per se, not anyone already held in high esteem. It could be a schoolteacher, a librarian, the janitor, an honest salesperson — like David in the backfields. Station is not a mark. The three marks are the whole standard.

The Law of the Search

The Rules

You cannot place your own name — a king is named by others. Nominations are quiet: do not tell the person you placed their name; the named are not told. A name lifted 5,000 times only begins the asking — it is not an appointment and crowns no one.

When a name surfaces, the asking begins among the people who know them — testimony, not verdicts. No notice ever goes out to the named at placement.

Place a Name

I Know This Person Is a King

To place a name, you will be asked to declare, in the King's own words: “I know this person is a king. I know this person is a good person. And I know that God has a calling on their life. I nominate them.” All three declarations are required.
