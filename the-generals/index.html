From the First Page

The Kingdom of Dgireahia begins with the Secret Covenant of the Chairman, who decreed an illusion so vast it would escape perception, using soft metals, aging accelerators, and poisons in food and air to keep the masses weak and separated by dogma, declaring that if the people ever found out they were equal, the elites would perish.

The Kingdom answers this with the Letter, sealed with the golden lion, the double-edged sword, the djed dandelions, the serpent beneath its feet, and the crown, engraved with John 17, carrying the response codes Ta'ki U, meaning I accept — and Un Mu'na Ta'ki U, meaning I will not.

Eight Generals receive this letter. District 2, Viktor Volkov in Moscow, stands on a balcony in the snow with a glass of untouched vodka, haunted by Chechnya in 1999 where he watched Colonel Morozov load children onto canvas-backed trucks, specifically a girl of eight or nine holding a stuffed rabbit by one ear who asked, "Where are we going, Uncle?" — and he said nothing, later learning her name was Anya and she never returned from the facility in the mountains.

That is only the first page. Eight Generals. Eight districts. A Letter, sealed — and a code that opens one letter at a time. Part One of The Generals goes first to every new citizen of the Kingdom.
