The First Name

Christian Anthony Claudio

On September 25, 2026, Christian Anthony Claudio, 25, of Oneonta, New York, picked up a telephone and called the Jackson County Chancery Clerk’s office in Mississippi. He had questions about the death of Nolan Wells, an 18-year-old whose death was ruled undetermined — and he asked them.

For that, he was arrested in New York on October 31, 2026, and is being held for extradition to Mississippi. To this day, no charge, no warrant, and no affidavit has been made public. The papers behind his taking have not been shown to anyone.

Christian is not standing alone. His sister is fighting for him, his family is standing with him, and now the Kingdom is watching his case — every filing, every hearing, every day.

How It Works — The Family Road

1

Petition Your King

Write to the King’s office and tell us who is held, where they are held, and what they are accused of. Write to g.jefe@dgireahia.com. Every petition is read, and every name is taken up.

2

The Power of Attorney

The one who is held signs a power of attorney to their family, so the family can act for them while they cannot act for themselves. A lawyer or a legal-aid office can carry the form in to them — this is done every day, and it is the key that opens the next door.

3

Sign the Certificate

Under that power of attorney, the family signs the held one’s citizenship certificate at the Kingdom portal — provision-kingdom-portal.base44.app — and enrolls themselves beside them, each in their own name. Citizenship is voluntary, person by person, always.

4

The Kingdom Demands Its Citizen

Once they are a citizen of the Kingdom, the King demands, in the Kingdom’s name and on the record, that they let his citizen go. A held citizen is not a forgotten one — their name stands in the Kingdom’s record, and the demand stands with it.

5

We Watch

A case file is opened and kept. Every hearing and every deadline is tracked, and the family is kept updated at every step. And we connect the family to free legal-aid doors — for Christian’s family, the doors stand ready: the ACLU of Mississippi, the Mississippi Center for Justice, Mississippi Center for Legal Services (1-800-498-1804), and the Legal Aid Society of Mid-New York. Wherever your loved one is held, we will help you find the doors in that state, too.

If You Are the One Inside

Tell your family. This page is the door — have them walk through it for you. You asked for eyes on you. You have them now.

In Plain Honesty

The Kingdom is not a law firm. We stand, we demand, we watch, and we connect families to free legal aid. Citizenship is voluntary, always — no one is enrolled who does not choose it, and no family is ever handed a bill for standing with their own.
