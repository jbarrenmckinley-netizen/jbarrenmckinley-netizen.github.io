THE SEVEN PILLARS

The Kingdom of DGIREAH

Named by the King on October 19, 2026.

Health Care

Care that touches every family daily — held to a pure standard: water with its minerals in it, and products without the things that hurt people.

Housing

Homes for the living. Its first vehicle is the Tiny House Program — land and building, from the ground up.

Food

Food held to the pure product standard — no artificial flavorings, no Yellow 5 and Blue 6, nothing that hurts the people who eat it.

Innovation

“The innovative innovation” — new invention, and free-energy devices by the young, opening new areas of building.

Agriculture

Copper gardens and clean growing — food raised the clean way, on the land.

Energy (free)

Free energy — ending the chaos in that industry, so people move into new areas of building.

Reconciliation

The capstone, and the one ministry: reconciliation. The other six pillars serve this one — every citizen of one family, under one Father.
